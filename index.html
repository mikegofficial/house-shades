How All 4 Edges Disappear Into the Wall

Standard shades hang inside finished drywall, forcing a 3/4" clearance gap on both sides so the fabric doesn't scrape. Because we're building with 2×6 exterior walls, we build the tracks inside the framing before drywall goes up:

1. Top — Recessed Ceiling Pocket (4" × 4") The motor and roller tube sit completely above the ceiling plane with a flush closure flap. When raised, you see 100% clear glass and zero roller hardware.
2. Left & Right — Flush-Recessed 2.5" Wool-Pile Channels Lutron’s extruded side channels get recessed into the 2×6 window jambs and mudded/trimmed flush. The blackout fabric rides inside dark wool-pile brushes—zero side sunbeams.
3. Bottom — Blackout Wool-Pile Hembar + Sill Angle Instead of a hard metal bar hovering over an uneven sill, a dense brush strip along the bottom bar seats into a slim sill angle at 100% closed.
4. Sneaky Light-Leak Catch — Pocket Doors 208 & 209 Sheet A2.3 currently specs 1'-6" glass transoms above the Closet (208) and Hallway (209) doors. Making those two transoms solid blocks 6:46 AM hall sun (H2-K1/L1) and 8:21 PM closet sun (PCL-O1).

Six Blueprint Catches We Flagged Before Framing

01
Vaulted Primary Ceiling Pockets (PRM-N1, PRM-M1/M2)

The Primary roof slopes down to ~9'-1" above PRM-N1 (9'×6' South slider) and 12'-0" above PRM-M1/M2. Framing recessed head pockets + 2.5" jamb channels must be drawn into the framing set now.

02
Make Pocket Door Transoms 208 & 209 Solid/Opaque

Hallway clerestories H2-K1/L1 get 6:46 AM East sun and Closet PCL-O1 gets 8:21 PM summer West sun. Omitting or frosting those two interior transoms keeps the bedroom pitch-black and saves ~$4,000 in extra shades.

03
Low-Profile Folding Cranks on Casements PRM-M1 & PRM-M2

Standard projecting casement cranks can bump blackout fabric inside recessed jamb channels. Specify folding/nested hardware on the window order.

04
Update Electrical Note #1 (16/2 Low-Voltage + 1 Dining 120V Outlet)

Pull 16/2 wire from every window pocket back to a closet/attic outlet (for plug-in / Altronix 12V power—no Lutron panel), plus 1 recessed 120V outlet tucked at one end of the curved DIN-D1..D4 track.

05
Frame ≥ 20" Wide Pockets Over 1'-6" (18") Narrow Windows

Windows A, B, and I are 18" wide—narrower than Lutron’s 19.5" Triathlon roller minimum. Making any ceiling pockets above them 20"+ wide keeps future shade options wide open.

06
Straight Pocket Inside 10' Ceiling Above Arched LIV-D108

Recessing a straight 4.5"×4.5" pocket in the flat 10' ceiling just inside the 12'×10' courtyard doors preserves the curved top corners 100% unobstructed whenever the shade is up.
